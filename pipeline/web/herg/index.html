    Interpretation boundary

    A research prediction is a hypothesis, not a safety determination.

    Use applicability and intervals alongside every point estimate. Low-domain predictions should be treated as high risk.

    The direct curve and literature IC50 are different assay domains and should not be averaged.

    The optional receptor-aware output combines ligand and receptor features, but remains an experimental diagnostic and does not alter the default.

    Results are not prospectively validated and are not intended for clinical, regulatory, or patient-care decisions.
